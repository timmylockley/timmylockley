Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before we start coding.

How much does a custom website cost?

Pricing varies greatly depending on the complexity of the project. A simple landing page starts at $1,000, while complex web applications can range from $5,000 to $20,000+. Contact us for a precise quote.

How long does a project take?

Standard websites typically take 3 to 6 weeks. More complex software and mobile apps can take 2 to 4 months of dedicated development time.

Do you provide ongoing maintenance?

Yes! We offer monthly retainer packages to keep your software updated, secure, and running smoothly.
